Sample work

Solid model of the former IRIB building, Urmia

58 separate solids, each structural element on its own layer, ready for meshing and material assignment in ANSYS. Everything was generated from the existing-condition drawings by an AutoLISP routine, so if a dimension changes, the model is rebuilt in about a minute.
