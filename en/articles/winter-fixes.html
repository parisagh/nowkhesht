3 cheap ways to a warmer home

Before you spend on insulation, do these three things. Each takes less than a day and costs little.

1. Cover the evaporative cooler vent

In winter, the duct of an evaporative (desert) cooler acts like an open window, and warm air escapes through it. Close the vents from inside and cover them with an insulating cover.

2. Seal windows and doors

Foam or rubber weatherstrip around the sashes stops cold draughts. To find gaps, hold a candle or incense stick next to the frame; wherever the flame or smoke moves, there is a gap.

3. Put reflective foil behind radiators

A radiator on an external wall sends part of its heat into the wall and outside. A reflective foil panel behind it sends the heat back into the room.

If a room is still cold after these three fixes, or mould appears in a ceiling corner, you probably have a thermal bridge or an uninsulated wall. A thermal camera survey shows exactly where the heat goes.
